Stripe

Stripe Partner Ecosystem

Partner types
Referral, Solution / SI, ISV / Embedded, Technology partner, Consulting partner
Tiers
Stripe Partner, Premier Partner
Revenue share model
Custom / negotiated. SaaS platforms using Stripe Connect are eligible for a 'revenue sharing' program on processing volume; Premier Partner (SaaS track) requires at least $1M in partnership revenue for buy-rate platforms or $300M in payment volume for referral-model platforms. Services-track partners earn referral/co-sell support rather than disclosed percentages. Exact rev-share percentages are not publicly disclosed. (Stripe SaaS platform partners docs)
FX markup transparency
Not publicly detailed for partners. Stripe discloses over 125+ global payment methods and currency support in its core payments docs, but does not publish standardized FX markup disclosure requirements for partners or end-customers on its partner pages. (Stripe Sessions 2025 product update)
Cross-border coverage
135+ currencies accepted; 40+ countries with full Stripe account support (with additional 'Preview' markets); 125+ local payment methods including stablecoins and real-time payment rails like Pix and UPI. (Stripe currencies docs; Stripe Sessions 2025 update; Red Stag Fulfillment country guide)
Embedded finance capabilities
Yes. Stripe Connect enables embedded payments, payouts, and marketplace/platform account management; Stripe also offers Issuing (card issuing), Treasury (embedded banking/accounts), and 'Managed Payments' (merchant-of-record offering announced 2025) covering tax compliance, fraud/disputes, and checkout. (Stripe Sessions 2025 update)
API-first
Yes. Stripe's entire partner ecosystem is built around API integration (Payment Intents, Connect, Checkout, Payment Element, webhooks). (Stripe SaaS platform partners docs)
Deal registration
Yes. Services-track Partner and Premier Partner tiers both have 'Deal registration to request co-selling support' as a stated benefit. (Stripe Services partners docs)
MDF available
Yes. Both Partner and Premier tiers in the Services track are eligible to request market development funds (MDF); Premier Partners in the SaaS track get MDF as an added benefit. (Stripe Services partners docs; Stripe SaaS platform partners docs)
Annual partner fee
No standard published fee for the core Partner Ecosystem; one third-party affiliate resource cites a legacy 'Stripe Verified Partner Program' with a $250/year fee, but this is not corroborated on Stripe's current official pages. (Wise blog on Stripe affiliate program)
Ecosystem size
Not disclosed as a specific partner count on official pages; described as 'thousands of trusted partners around the world.' (Stripe Partner Ecosystem page)
Best-fit ICP
SaaS platforms embedding payments

Adyen

Adyen Partner Program

Partner types
Technology partner, Consulting partner, Solution / SI
Tiers
Partner (badged/approved), Premier, Premier Plus
Revenue share model
Custom / negotiated. Technology partners 'may also be eligible for revenue-sharing opportunities based on merchant transaction volume'; Service partners can be eligible for implementation funds; a separate referral program rewards partners for bringing new customers or leads. Specific percentages are not publicly disclosed. (Adyen Partner Program launch announcement)
FX markup transparency
Partial. Adyen markets 'transparent, straightforward' pricing without hidden fees for its Adyen for Platforms product (per third-party analysis), and for Dynamic Currency Conversion Adyen recently expanded support to 25 additional currencies, but Adyen's own partner pages do not detail FX markup disclosure requirements for partners or sub-merchants. (Adyen DCC currency expansion; Sharetribe overview of Adyen for Platforms)
Cross-border coverage
Adyen for Platforms supports payouts in 15+ currencies across 35 countries; broader Adyen acquiring supports payment acceptance in nearly 100 countries with a single platform/API approach across geographies and channels. (Sharetribe overview of Adyen for Platforms; Adyen supported countries by product)
Embedded finance capabilities
Yes — one of the most comprehensive in the set. Adyen's Embedded Finance suite includes four connected products: Embedded Payments, Embedded Issuing (branded card issuing with interchange revenue), Embedded Accounts (instant access to settled funds), and Embedded Capital (instant loans up to $100k with Adyen absorbing credit risk). (Adyen embedded finance guide)
API-first
Yes. Adyen is built on a 'single platform and single API' approach across geographies and channels (per Forrester Wave Q1 2024, cited on Adyen's own partner page). (Adyen Partners page)
Deal registration
Not explicitly disclosed as 'deal registration' on public pages, though the program includes referral rewards and co-selling support structures. (Adyen Partner Program launch announcement)
MDF available
Yes. Top-tier partners gain access to dedicated marketing support and market development funds (MDF) to fuel joint campaigns. (Adyen Partner Program launch announcement)
Annual partner fee
Not publicly disclosed.
Ecosystem size
Not disclosed as a specific number on official pages; third-party partner-tracking site Partnerbase lists dozens of named technology/channel partners including Microsoft, Salesforce, SAP, Adobe, Oracle, and Shopify. (Partnerbase Adyen partnerships)

Wise Platform

Wise Platform (embedded) / Wise Business

Partner types
ISV / Embedded, Technology partner
Tiers
—
Revenue share model
Custom / negotiated for embedding partners (banks, enterprises, platforms); not structured as a public partner rev-share/tier program like Stripe or Adyen. Wise's own end-customer pricing is transparent: average fee of 0.53% globally as of December 2025, down from 0.59% a year earlier, using the mid-market rate with zero FX markup. (Wise December 2025 fee review; FXC Intelligence Wise investor day insights)
FX markup transparency
Yes — Wise's core differentiator. Wise uses the real mid-market exchange rate with 0% markup and charges a separate, transparent fee (fixed + variable, ~0.33%-0.65% depending on corridor) disclosed upfront before every transfer; this transparency extends to Wise Platform's embedded offering for partner banks/enterprises. (Wise mid-market rate explainer; Wise December 2025 fee review)
Cross-border coverage
160+ countries; 40+ currencies; direct access to domestic payment systems in 8 markets (Australia, Brazil, Europe/SEPA, Hungary, Japan, Philippines, Singapore, UK). Cross-border volume reached £181.7bn in FY26 (+25% YoY), with a Q4 FY26 take rate of 51 basis points. (Wise Platform page; Wise FY26 results coverage)
Embedded finance capabilities
Yes. Wise Platform lets banks, enterprises, and platforms embed send/receive money, card issuing, multi-currency accounts, customizable onboarding/KYC, and Swift messaging integration via the Wise Platform API. (Wise Platform page)
API-first
Yes. Wise Platform explicitly offers connection 'via Swift or API' with full API documentation for partners. (Wise Platform page)
Deal registration
Not publicly disclosed (Wise Platform is a direct enterprise/bank partnership model rather than a classic channel-partner program).
MDF available
Not publicly disclosed.
Annual partner fee
Not publicly disclosed; embedding partners negotiate commercial terms directly with Wise.
Ecosystem size
70+ licenses worldwide; 950+ engineers; >99% straight-through-processing (STP) rate; 74% of instant payments complete in under 20 seconds and 96% in under 24 hours. Wise Platform accounted for roughly 9% of total Wise volume as of FY2023 per third-party analysis, with continued growth since. (Wise Platform page; LinkedIn/FintechWrapUp on Wise Platform volume share

Insights

0 of 11 payment programs publicly disclose exact rev-share percentages

Every vendor in this tracker — from Stripe to Convera — describes partner revenue share as "custom," "negotiated," or purely qualitative language like "earn commissions on volume." Even the most detailed public disclosures, such as Stripe's SaaS platform partner docs (which publish dollar-value tier thresholds of $1M in partnership revenue or $300M in payment volume for Premier status) and Adyen's three-tier structure, stop short of naming an actual percentage. Buyers and prospective partners cannot comparison-shop rev-share terms without a direct sales conversation.

Why this matters for partnerships: expect a discovery call before you can model your own economics — build extra time into partner evaluation cycles.

Only Wise guarantees a quantified 0% FX markup

Wise is the sole vendor in this set to publish a hard, quantified FX transparency commitment: the real mid-market rate with 0% markup, plus a running average all-in fee of about 0.53% globally as of December 2025, down from 0.59% a year earlier (Wise December 2025 fee review; Wise mid-market rate explainer). The other ten providers either stay silent on FX markup entirely or use unquantified language such as "transparent, straightforward pricing" — Adyen and Payoneer fall into this partial-disclosure middle ground.

Why this matters for partnerships: if FX transparency is a selling point for your end customers, Wise Platform is currently the only vendor whose marketing claim is independently verifiable.

7 of 11 providers now have stablecoin-enabled rails or settlement capabilities

Stablecoin infrastructure moved from experimental to embedded-partner-strategy territory: Stripe expanded Treasury, payouts, and platform wallet rails (Stripe Sessions 2026); Rapyd launched Stablecoin Payment Solutions (Rapyd); Nium added Visa, Circle, and Coinbase settlement paths (Nium/Coinbase); dLocal launched Stablecoin Full across 44+ emerging markets (dLocal); Convera partnered with Ripple (Convera/Ripple); Payoneer announced Bridge-powered capabilities (Payoneer); and Corpay added BVNK/J.P. Morgan blockchain settlement and wallets (Corpay/BVNK).

Why this matters for partnerships: partners building multi-year integrations should ask each vendor directly about stablecoin settlement roadmaps — this is now a live competitive axis, not a future one.

Payoneer's partner program is a moving target mid-acquisition

Nuvei's announced $2.75 billion all-cash acquisition of Payoneer ($7.40/share) remains pending: Payoneer's July 7 materials say shareholder and regulatory approvals are outstanding, an estimated shareholder vote is expected around August–September 2026, and closing remains targeted for mid-2027; Payoneer remains independent and Nasdaq-listed until close (Payoneer DEFA14A). Partners evaluating Payoneer's Integration Partnerships or Affiliate Program today should factor in likely program consolidation once the deal closes.

Why this matters for partnerships: negotiate contract terms with an eye toward the post-acquisition entity — ask specifically what happens to existing partner agreements after close.
