Stripe

Stripe Partner Ecosystem

Partner types
Referral, Solution / SI, ISV / Embedded, Technology partner, Consulting partner
Tiers
Stripe Partner, Premier Partner
Revenue share model
Custom / negotiated. SaaS platforms using Stripe Connect are eligible for a 'revenue sharing' program on processing volume; Premier Partner (SaaS track) requires at least $1M in partnership revenue for buy-rate platforms or $300M in payment volume for referral-model platforms. Services-track partners earn referral/co-sell support rather than disclosed percentages. Exact rev-share percentages are not publicly disclosed. (Stripe SaaS platform partners docs)
FX markup transparency
Not publicly detailed for partners. Stripe discloses over 125+ global payment methods and currency support in its core payments docs, but does not publish standardized FX markup disclosure requirements for partners or end-customers on its partner pages. (Stripe Sessions 2025 product update)
Cross-border coverage
135+ currencies accepted and 40+ countries with full Stripe account support. The October 2026 update adds instant stablecoin payouts to 160 markets and stablecoin card issuing in 27 EU countries, bringing availability to 65+ countries worldwide; Stripe plans to expand card issuing to 100 countries by year-end 2026. The source does not state that the planned 100-country expansion is already live. (Stripe supported currencies; Stripe October 2026 update)
Embedded finance capabilities
Yes. Connect, Treasury, Issuing, and Global Payouts can be combined for platform-fintech products. Global Payouts can be embedded through hosted forms or APIs; the October 2026 update adds stablecoin-funded card programs using custodial or noncustodial wallets or Stripe financial accounts, with automatic local-currency conversion at purchase. (Stripe October 2026 update; Stripe OUSD launch)
API-first
Yes. Stripe's entire partner ecosystem is built around API integration (Payment Intents, Connect, Checkout, Payment Element, webhooks). (Stripe SaaS platform partners docs)
Deal registration
Yes. Services-track Partner and Premier Partner tiers both have 'Deal registration to request co-selling support' as a stated benefit. (Stripe Services partners docs)
MDF available
Yes. Both Partner and Premier tiers in the Services track are eligible to request market development funds (MDF); Premier Partners in the SaaS track get MDF as an added benefit. (Stripe Services partners docs; Stripe SaaS platform partners docs)
Annual partner fee
No standard published fee for the core Partner Ecosystem; one third-party affiliate resource cites a legacy 'Stripe Verified Partner Program' with a $250/year fee, but this is not corroborated on Stripe's current official pages. (Wise blog on Stripe affiliate program)
Ecosystem size
Not disclosed as a specific partner count on official pages; described as 'thousands of trusted partners around the world.' (Stripe Partner Ecosystem page)
Best-fit ICP
SaaS platforms embedding payments

Wise Platform

Wise Platform (embedded) / Wise Business

Partner types
ISV / Embedded, Technology partner
Tiers
None publicly disclosed
Revenue share model
Custom / negotiated for embedding partners (banks, enterprises, platforms); not structured as a public partner rev-share/tier program like Stripe or Adyen. Yes — Wise's core differentiator. (Wise Platform; Wise/OakNorth; Wise/Vivid)
Embedded finance capabilities
Yes. Wise Platform lets banks, enterprises, and platforms embed send/receive money, card issuing, multi-currency accounts, customizable onboarding/KYC, and Swift messaging integration via the Wise Platform API. (Wise Platform page)
API-first
Yes. Wise Platform explicitly offers connection 'via Swift or API' with full API documentation for partners. (Wise Platform page)
Deal registration
Not publicly disclosed (Wise Platform is a direct enterprise/bank partnership model rather than a classic channel-partner program).
MDF available
Not publicly disclosed.
Annual partner fee
Not publicly disclosed; embedding partners negotiate commercial terms directly with Wise.
Ecosystem size
70+ licenses worldwide; 950+ engineers; Wise FY26 added Platform partners including UniCredit, Raiffeisen Bank, MBSB Bank, and Capitec; Q2 2026 expanded Raiffeisen Bank International and GoTyme Bank partnerships and added Raiffeisen Bank Kosovo. (Wise Platform page; Wise FY26 results; Wise Platform Q2 2026 update)

Insights

0 of 11 payment programs publicly disclose exact rev-share percentages

Every vendor in this tracker — from Stripe to Convera — describes partner revenue share as "custom," "negotiated," or purely qualitative language like "earn commissions on volume." Even the most detailed public disclosures, such as Stripe's SaaS platform partner docs (which publish dollar-value tier thresholds of $1M in partnership revenue or $300M in payment volume for Premier status) and Adyen's three-tier structure, stop short of naming an actual percentage. Buyers and prospective partners cannot comparison-shop rev-share terms without a direct sales conversation.

Why this matters for partnerships: expect a discovery call before you can model your own economics — build extra time into partner evaluation cycles.

Only Wise guarantees a quantified 0% FX markup

Wise is the sole vendor in this set to publish a hard, quantified FX transparency commitment: the real mid-market rate with 0% markup, plus a running average all-in fee of about 0.53% globally as of December 2025, down from 0.59% a year earlier (Wise December 2025 fee review; Wise mid-market rate explainer). The other ten providers either stay silent on FX markup entirely or use unquantified language such as "transparent, straightforward pricing" — Adyen and Payoneer fall into this partial-disclosure middle ground.

Why this matters for partnerships: if FX transparency is a selling point for your end customers, Wise Platform is currently the only vendor whose marketing claim is independently verifiable.

7 of 11 tracked providers have live stablecoin-enabled rails; new reach is expanding

Stablecoin capabilities are expanding, but announcements do not all mean a live integration. Stripe announced instant stablecoin payouts to 160 markets and stablecoin card availability in 65+ countries, with 100 countries planned by year-end (Stripe October update). Nium announced its off-ramp partnership with RedotPay, but gave no rollout date or markets (Nium/RedotPay). Mastercard and BVNK launched Global Money Platform, available through BVNK; Nuvei is among the first companies that will integrate, but the release does not say Nuvei is live (Mastercard/BVNK). That planned Nuvei integration is separate from the pending Payoneer/Nuvei acquisition. Airwallex has described work with Metal on stablecoin support in regulated markets, but has not announced a customer product launch or date (Airwallex/Metal).

Why this matters for partnerships: partners building multi-year integrations should ask each vendor directly about stablecoin settlement roadmaps — this is now a live competitive axis, not a future one.

Payoneer's partner program is a moving target mid-acquisition

The announced $2.75 billion all-cash acquisition remains pending. Payoneer’s October 22 investor release again described the transaction as pending and announced no closing or regulatory approvals; it gave no new closing timeframe (Payoneer October 22 release). Payoneer shareholders approved the deal on September 14, but Payoneer remains independent until closing (September 14 Form 8-K). Its current partner page documents four tiers, with custom revenue share only named for Platinum and no exact rates or thresholds (Payoneer partner page). Separately, Nuvei is named as a planned initial integrator of Mastercard/BVNK Global Money Platform; this is not a Payoneer product announcement and is not stated to be live (Mastercard/BVNK).

Why this matters for partnerships: negotiate contract terms with an eye toward the post-acquisition entity — ask specifically what happens to existing partner agreements after close.
